Health & safety

Photosensitivity / epilepsy warning

CRT SIM can produce flashing lights, rapid contrast changes, and moving patterns that may trigger seizures or discomfort in people with photosensitive epilepsy or other visual sensitivities.

Stop using the application immediately if you feel unwell. Viewer discretion is advised.
