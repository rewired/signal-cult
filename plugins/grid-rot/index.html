Health & safety

Photosensitivity / epilepsy warning

GRID ROT can produce flashing lights, rapid contrast changes and moving patterns that may trigger seizures or discomfort in people with photosensitive epilepsy or other visual sensitivities.

Temporal strobing and rapid local frame changes can intensify these effects. Stop using the application if you feel unwell.
